Page-Site Ambiguity

1 AC5

A site's friendly URL matches a top-level page on the default site. After the public friendly URL mapping is disabled, only one of them can be reached at the same path.

Site /marketing conflicts with default-site page /marketing

Default site: Liferay · Conflicting site: Marketing EMEA
High
Once /web/ is dropped, requests to example.com/marketing will resolve to the site (Marketing EMEA), and the default-site page Marketing will become unreachable at that URL. Consider renaming one of them, or use a virtual host on the site to disambiguate.

Engineering question (per the story): Sites with virtual host configurations also serve pages at root paths on their hostname and have the same ambiguity. Confirm whether to fold those into AC5 or treat as a follow-up story.

Hardcoded /web/ References

2 AC6

Templates that hardcode /web/ in URLs will produce broken links after the prefix is removed. Update them to use themeDisplay.getPathFriendlyURL() or the <portal:url /> tag instead.
